# Pilot data card

**Scope:** original source material and reviewed v1.2 derived products used by the Predictive Atmospheres Research Atlas. The repository is already public, and the owner explicitly authorized public recordings, metadata, renders and results. This card documents content and limits; it does not grant a blanket third-party license or establish permission to link participant names to biometric recordings.

## Source and collection

The retained canonical source inventory contains 160 raw `Counter,Channel1,Channel2,Channel3` CSV recordings, 7 metadata CSVs, 30 Lumion room renders and 116 historical website images. Current paths and original-file SHA-256 hashes are in `data/source-inventory.json` and `data/MANIFEST.sha256`; the [October source check](reports/revision-2026-10/source-inventory.md) retains hashes and Windows-original verification for thesis documents removed from the project tree. The original migration path map remains in [phase 0](reports/phase-0.md). The experiments cover 50, 50 and 60 room-viewing trials respectively, with five, five and six participant appearances; repeated IDs across experiments refer to the same people, giving ten distinct IDs in the generated people export. Thirty room IDs map to preserved render filenames.

`Subject Data.csv` supplies age and gender for all ten matching source IDs; the export validates this exact ID mapping and omits occupation. Sleep hours are recorded on all 60 Experiment 3 trial rows and are structurally unavailable in Experiments 1 and 2. The people product retains experiment-specific trial and complete-fusion counts, means and sleep observation count/mean/range. A participant-wide sleep value remains unavailable because recording scope differs across experiments. These fields are descriptive source observations, not evidence of demographic effects or anonymous identity.

The investigator reports data collection in Barcelona using an Upside Down Labs NPG Lite with Chords, approximate right/left forehead electrodes and a wrist ECG channel. Participants stood and turned while viewing non-interactive 360-degree Lumion rooms in a Meta Quest 3 gallery. The original files do **not** establish a hardware sampling rate, channel physical units, exact electrode reference, event timestamps or neutral baseline. The counter wraps modulo 256 and is an integrity field, not a confirmed time clock. [Acquisition provenance](protocol/acquisition.md) separates report from source observation; [spatial provenance](protocol/spatial-provenance.md) explains that supplied light and geometry fields were derived in Grasshopper/Ladybug/Honeybee and not matched to measured headset exposure.

Ratings differ by experiment. Experiment 1's comfort score is its own construct and must not be relabelled valence. Experiments 2 and 3 provide signed valence/arousal values through different reported response procedures; their measurement equivalence is unresolved. Original forms and conversion sheets are unavailable. See [rating provenance](protocol/ratings.md). No absent questionnaire becomes a collected measure.

## Processing and eligible cohorts

The [approved v1.2 analysis specification](specs/analysis-v1.2-draft.md) declares conditional 500 Hz processing with alternative-rate sensitivity at 250, 256 and 512 Hz. The candidate rate is not a recovered device setting. CP-B reviewed all 346 channel/trial queue entries using source-hashed machine evidence and selected panels; its delegated agent dispositions are retained in [CP-B](reports/revision-2026-10/CP-B.md) and `artifacts/results/qc_review.json`. Review preserves invalid, uncertain or missing components as unavailable; accepted EEG features do not prove an artifact-free trace. Across 160 trials, reviewed timebase is eligible in 154, bilateral EEG in 128, HR in 66, and the separate 30-second RMSSD rule in 17. By experiment, bilateral EEG/HR/RMSSD are 50/13/0 in E1, 37/20/4 in E2, and 41/33/13 in E3. These are feature counts, not independent people or proof of physiological validity.

Descriptive fusion uses observed self-report and reviewed eligible components without replacing missing terms with neutral values. Within-person median/MAD calibration requires at least three distinct eligible trials with positive scale. Experiment 2 contributes 19 complete-fusion trials; Experiment 3 contributes 21. The 40 complete descriptive positions come from five distinct people across those experiments. E2 also has 18 partial-modality, one partial-axis-overlap and 12 self-report-only trials; E3 has 20 partial-modality, 14 partial-axis-overlap and five self-report-only trials. Experiment 1 has no self-reported valence/arousal and remains 50 objective-only records, separate from affect and prediction. The E3 prediction cohort is different: 23 raw full-component trials across four people and ten rooms use training-fold population calibration, never globally normalized descriptive targets. RMSSD is descriptive sensitivity only and is not required for model inclusion. See the [model card](model_card.md).

## Product surfaces and limitations

`artifacts/results/` contains the source audit, conditional signal detail and rate scenarios, reviewed eligibility, affect detail/analysis, validity, rating research, self-report comparator, grouped model evaluation, model controls, six versioned website products (`study`, `rooms`, `signals`, `affect`, `people`, `model`), the joined `bundle`, and a hash manifest. Its `analysis/` catalogue provides 28 versioned, validated products S1–S5, R1–R9, B1–B8, P1–P5 and Methods, including explicit unavailable products. The website bundle includes metadata but no full waveform arrays; the anti-aliased browser Signals product is fetched only on that route. Full-rate recordings and detailed analysis evidence stay outside the browser product. Browser source-render previews are derived WebP images; the original JPEG/PNG room render files are retained, and the top-eye extraction from stacked stereo renders is explicitly an **inferred presentation choice**.

The bundle includes global and participant-scoped alpha sensitivity and physiology-minus-report disagreement summaries, each with available-record denominators. It also supplies complete original E3 independent room attributes for Studio presets, without filling absent E1/E2 fields. Experiment 1 comfort remains a separate trial field, never an affect axis. The model product publishes generated cohort, exclusion, preprocessing, selection and artifact-provenance evidence with links to the retained evaluation and card.

Participants and rooms are repeatedly observed and crossed. Sparse independent people, protocol differences, uncertain acquisition settings, unverified environmental exposure and reviewed but still approximate ECG/EEG QC limit inferential claims. A trial count is not a population sample size. No default confidence interval or demographic effect is claimed by the static atlas. The observed fused E3 model did not beat its room-held-out median baseline and cannot be used for clinical, health, comfort or design guarantees.

## Access, ownership and updates

Participant IDs in generated outputs are source-derived codes, not proof of anonymization. The public repository and owner authorization for public research files are existing facts, not pending permission questions. No verified mapping from participant names to source IDs or specific permission to publish named biometric records was supplied, so the implementation retains IDs; author credits are separate. Code, source data, renders and documents still require separate license decisions, including any third-party material; this card grants none. Do not attach an inherited DOI or publish named biometric associations based on historical roadmap text alone. [Release readiness](release-readiness.md) tracks these open decisions.

Update this card after any new acquisition evidence, source correction, approved processing amendment or cohort change. Preserve original bytes and rerun affected products with the frozen pipeline; compare the new manifest and document changed eligibility and model evaluation before changing public claims.
