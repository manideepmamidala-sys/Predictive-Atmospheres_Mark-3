# Pilot data card

**Scope:** original source material and derived products used by the 2026-10-08 Predictive Atmospheres rebuild. The repository is already public, and the owner explicitly authorized public recordings, metadata, renders and results. This card documents content and limits; it does not grant a blanket third-party license or establish permission to link participant names to biometric recordings.

## Source and collection

The preserved source inventory contains 160 raw `Counter,Channel1,Channel2,Channel3` CSV recordings, 7 metadata CSVs, 30 Lumion room renders, 116 historical website images and 2 thesis PDFs. Original-file SHA-256 hashes are in `data/MANIFEST.sha256`; preservation paths and byte checks are recorded in [phase 0](reports/phase-0.md). The experiments cover 50, 50 and 60 room-viewing trials respectively, with five, five and six participant appearances; repeated IDs across experiments refer to the same people, giving ten distinct IDs in the generated people export. Thirty room IDs map to preserved render filenames.

`Subject Data.csv` supplies age and gender for all ten matching source IDs; the export validates this exact ID mapping and omits occupation. Sleep hours are recorded on all 60 Experiment 3 trial rows and are structurally unavailable in Experiments 1 and 2. The people product retains experiment-specific trial and complete-fusion counts, means and sleep observation count/mean/range. A participant-wide sleep value remains unavailable because recording scope differs across experiments. These fields are descriptive source observations, not evidence of demographic effects or anonymous identity.

The investigator reports data collection in Barcelona using an Upside Down Labs NPG Lite with Chords, approximate right/left forehead electrodes and a wrist ECG channel. Participants stood and turned while viewing non-interactive 360-degree Lumion rooms in a Meta Quest 3 gallery. The original files do **not** establish a hardware sampling rate, channel physical units, exact electrode reference, event timestamps or neutral baseline. The counter wraps modulo 256 and is an integrity field, not a confirmed time clock. [Acquisition provenance](protocol/acquisition.md) separates report from source observation; [spatial provenance](protocol/spatial-provenance.md) explains that supplied light and geometry fields were derived in Grasshopper/Ladybug/Honeybee and not matched to measured headset exposure.

Ratings differ by experiment. Experiment 1's comfort score is its own construct and must not be relabelled valence. Experiments 2 and 3 provide signed valence/arousal values through different reported response procedures; their measurement equivalence is unresolved. Original forms and conversion sheets are unavailable. See [rating provenance](protocol/ratings.md). No absent questionnaire becomes a collected measure.

## Processing and eligible cohorts

The [analysis specification](specs/analysis-v1.md) declares conditional 500 Hz processing with alternative-rate sensitivity at 250, 256 and 512 Hz. The candidate rate is not a recovered device setting. Signal QC records file/epoch reasons and preserves invalid or missing features as unavailable. Accepted EEG features do not prove an artifact-free trace. Under the primary scenario, EEG features are operationally available for 160 trials; heart rate for 71; and the stricter 30-second RMSSD for 18. These numbers are feature counts, not independent people or proof of physiological validity.

Descriptive fusion uses observed self-report and eligible components without replacing missing terms with neutral values. Experiment 2 contributes 4 complete-fusion trials from one participant and 46 partial-modality trials; Experiment 3 contributes 10 complete-fusion trials from one participant and 50 partial-modality trials. Experiment 1 has no self-reported valence/arousal and contributes neither complete nor partial descriptive fusion. The 14 complete **descriptive** positions come from only two people across Experiments 2 and 3. Source-level Experiment 3 has 14 raw full-component trials from three people; four fail the separate within-person descriptive calibration rule. The prediction model's population-constructed target uses those 14 Experiment 3 trials under training-fold fitting, not the globally normalized descriptive values. See [phase 3](reports/phase-3.md) and the [model card](model_card.md).

## Product surfaces and limitations

`artifacts/results/` contains the source audit, conditional signal detail and rate scenarios, affect detail/analysis, grouped model evaluation, six versioned website products (`study`, `rooms`, `signals`, `affect`, `people`, `model`), a small joined `bundle`, and a hash manifest. The website's bundle includes metadata but no waveform arrays; the anti-aliased browser Signals product is fetched only on that route. Full-rate recordings and detailed analysis evidence stay outside the browser product. Browser source-render previews are derived WebP images; the original JPEGs are retained, and the top-eye extraction from stacked stereo renders is explicitly an **inferred presentation choice**.

The bundle includes global and participant-scoped alpha sensitivity and objective-minus-subjective disagreement summaries, each with available-record denominators. Experiment 1 comfort remains a separate trial field, never an affect axis. The model product publishes generated cohort, exclusion, preprocessing, selection and artifact-provenance evidence with links to the retained evaluation and card.

Participants and rooms are repeatedly observed and crossed. Sparse independent people, protocol differences, uncertain acquisition settings, unverified environmental exposure and automated ECG/EEG QC limit inferential claims. A trial count is not a population sample size. No default confidence interval or demographic effect is claimed by the static atlas. The model did not beat its room-held-out median baseline and cannot be used for clinical, health, comfort or design guarantees.

## Access, ownership and updates

Participant IDs in generated outputs are source-derived codes, not proof of anonymization. The public repository and owner authorization for public research files are existing facts, not pending permission questions. No verified mapping from participant names to source IDs or specific permission to publish named biometric records was supplied, so the implementation retains IDs; author credits are separate. Code, source data, renders and documents still require separate license decisions, including any third-party material; this card grants none. Do not attach an inherited DOI or publish named biometric associations based on historical roadmap text alone. [Release readiness](release-readiness.md) tracks these open decisions.

Update this card after any new acquisition evidence, source correction, approved processing amendment or cohort change. Preserve original bytes and rerun affected products with the frozen pipeline; compare the new manifest and document changed eligibility and model evaluation before changing public claims.
