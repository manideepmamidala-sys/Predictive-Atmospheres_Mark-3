# Acquisition and exposure protocol

**Provenance:** investigator report supplied 2026-10-08, checked against available files where stated. These details correct conflicting historical thesis prose; no original acquisition settings, event log or reference-wiring diagram was supplied.

The study took place in Barcelona. The investigator reports using an Upside Down Labs NPG Lite through Chords for all three experiments. Channel1 was attached near the right forehead (approximately Fp2), Channel2 near the left forehead (approximately Fp1), and Channel3 was wrist ECG. This is approximate bilateral forehead placement, not verified 10–20 localization. The investigator described an ear attachment behind the ear at the earlobe; its exact reference/ground role and side were not established. Signal amplitude units, firmware/app version and hardware sample-rate setting are unknown.

Participants stood and turned while viewing non-interactive 360-degree Lumion room renders in a Meta Quest 3 gallery. Recording began when the participant opened their eyes in the headset and stopped before headset removal. Afterwards the participant sat with closed eyes and gave ratings; those transitions and ratings are outside raw recordings. Exposures were intended to be around one minute, but actual logged and sampled lengths vary. No rest or neutral-room baseline recordings were collected. The researcher reports choosing presentation order randomly; filenames support an observed order but do not verify the randomization algorithm or event timestamps. Repeated subject IDs across experiments refer to the same people.

Raw CSVs contain `Counter,Channel1,Channel2,Channel3`, with no timestamp or physical-unit header. The counter is analyzed modulo 256 as an acquisition integrity signal; it is not independently a sample clock or a proven packet-loss count. The file-level audit in `artifacts/results/audit.json` distinguishes observed sample counts, logged times and conditional rate scenarios.
