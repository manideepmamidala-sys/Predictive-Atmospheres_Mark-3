# Experimental fused room-coordinate model card

**Status: `baseline_only`.** The fitted E3 Studio artifact selects a constant training-mean predictor. It is a retrospective demonstrator of a declared analysis pipeline, not evidence that room attributes predict emotion, health, comfort or design quality. Its output is a pair of constructed signed coordinates, not measured emotional ground truth. The [approved v1.2 specification](specs/analysis-v1.2-draft.md) fixes the method; [CP-B](reports/revision-2026-10/CP-B.md) records delegated quality dispositions, and the model comparison remains subject to the concrete CP-C evidence record.

## Source cohort and target

The fitted cohort has **23 E3 trials, four people and ten rooms**. All have source-supplied full independent room inputs, original valence/arousal ratings and reviewed eligible raw FAA, alpha suppression, engagement and heart rate. The reviewed component gates are independent: HR is eligible in 66 of 160 source trials, RMSSD in 17, and bilateral EEG in 128. RMSSD is a separate descriptive sensitivity and is **not** required for this predictor. E1 comfort is a separate rating question; E2 room fields and response procedure do not support the E3 full-input deployable model. Invalid or absent physiology is not replaced by nominal values.

The target is constructed inside each training partition: robust population median/MAD calibration of raw forehead FAA, negative log eligible-channel alpha power, log beta/(alpha+theta) engagement and HR, followed by bounded signed mapping. FAA supplies physiology-derived valence. Alpha suppression and engagement contribute equally to EEG arousal; EEG arousal and HR arousal contribute equally to physiology-derived arousal. The physiology-derived coordinate and original E3 signed report each receive weight 0.5. Descriptive within-person coordinates and partial-modality records never enter the model target. The map encodes a hypothesis and depends on unconfirmed signal acquisition settings; no clinical or individual-emotion meaning follows from a number on this plane.

## Fitting and held-out evidence

The same `RoomInput` and derived-feature builder serve training, direct inference, CLI, API and constrained search. Numeric imputation, missingness indicators, scaling, categorical encoding and target calibration are trained only on the training side of each split. The frozen candidate list contains mean/median dummy baselines, three Ridge penalties and four bounded 100-tree RandomForest settings. Three-fold grouped inner selection never uses outer outcomes. Ten leave-one-room-out and four leave-one-person-out outer folds answer different questions. Fold memberships, candidate scores, constructed held-out targets, selected and both baseline predictions, and outer-training calibrators are in [the evaluation artifact](../artifacts/results/model_evaluation.json).

Equal-axis, equal-held-out-room MAE is **0.34964**, versus **0.34606** for the median baseline and **0.34044** for the mean baseline. Equal-held-out-person MAE is **0.35118**, equal to the median fallback because the remaining person groups are too few for the approved inner selection. The final full-data room-group selection chooses `dummy_mean`; its output therefore does not respond to room attributes. No spatial gain, calibrated uncertainty radius or personal accuracy is demonstrated. These MAEs are on constructed-coordinate units, not source rating units. The separate self-report-only room comparison uses a different target and is research-only: E2 MAE **0.27373** and E3 MAE **0.48935** under their respective feature/response scope; it is not an alternative Studio fit.

The prespecified [learning artifact](../artifacts/results/model_learning_curve.json) evaluates all **455 unique** training-room subsets at k=4–9 (100, 100, 100, 100, 45 and 10 subsets). All were evaluable; mean selected MAE by k is 0.3760, 0.3582, 0.3530, 0.3420, 0.3427 and 0.3496. The corresponding median-baseline MAE is lower at every k, so this curve does not show a spatial learning gain. In the separate [spatial control artifact](../artifacts/results/model_null.json), all **1,000** prescribed whole-training-room attribute shuffles were fully refitted and evaluated. Their median room MAE is **0.34900**, with descriptive 2.5–97.5% bounds of **0.33348–0.41018**; the observed **0.34964** lies within that range. These overlapping controls are descriptive, not a calibrated population significance test. The [CP-C record](reports/revision-2026-10/CP-C.md) interprets both before public model findings are staged. Overlapping rooms/people and the tiny number of independent groups prevent a deployment-performance claim.

## Inference and score contract

The trusted local fitted scikit-learn pipeline is `artifacts/model/model.joblib`, with byte and dependency provenance in `metadata.json`. Startup and requests do not fit. The loader rejects incompatible data, specification, decisions, lockfile, source-tree or model bytes before trusted deserialization. `base_git_revision` is only the committed base when local changes exist; it does not assert that old HEAD reproduces the fitted model. Missing or malformed artifact evidence leaves process health live with `ready=false` and structured unavailable prediction/search responses.

Predict requires complete E3 independent room inputs; source E1/E2 missing fields are never structurally filled. It returns raw and clipped coordinates separately. The fixed Neuro-Score is `clip(1 − distance(predicted_point, user_target)/sqrt(8), 0, 1)`, a display convention rather than an empirical well-being scale. Physical validity and observed-support distance are separate checks. Support compares eleven observed numeric inputs and categorical combinations; it is a descriptive neighborhood, not a calibrated accuracy guarantee. The seeded bounded optimizer ranks feasible candidates by closeness to a requested **numerical score**, respects locks and ranges, and may return no solution. Neither flow promises an individual's response or an achievable emotional target.

## Limitations and update policy

The primary 500 Hz processing rate, electrode reference, physical signal units, EEG source localization and ECG normal-to-normal status are unconfirmed. Reviewed quality removes visibly or automatically flagged windows/components but does not establish artifact-free physiology. The room light and geometry fields came from simulations, not headset-exposure measurements. Participants and rooms repeat; E2/E3 rating procedures may differ. No causal effect, feature importance for the selected constant model, validated emotion measure or demographic inference is claimed. New acquisition evidence or an approved method change requires an append-only decision amendment, affected reruns, grouped reevaluation, artifact compatibility update and a revised card.
