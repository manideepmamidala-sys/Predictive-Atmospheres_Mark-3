## Estimands, units and cohorts (proposed PD-001)

The primary research description is how forehead EEG and wrist ECG features vary across the recorded room exposures, how the features compare with reported experience, and how constructed physiology/self-report/fused coordinates differ. Physiology gets chart and analysis priority, but the numerical fusion weight is a separate method choice. Neither a source feature nor a constructed coordinate is an independently validated emotion measure. Trial, distinct participant and distinct room denominators accompany every chart and statistic.

Keep E1 comfort as a separate 50-trial construct. E2 (50) and E3 (60) have valence/arousal ratings under different elicitation; report each experiment first. A pooled E2+E3 display is exploratory, includes an experiment indicator where modeled, and never implies measurement equivalence. Every one of the 160 records appears in an eligibility ledger with reasons for inclusion or exclusion in each analysis. Missing and rejected measurements stay missing. A fully observed source rating remains available to its eligible self-report analysis even if physiology fails. No trial is added or padded.

## Timebase and raw signal review (proposed PD-002)

Keep **500 Hz as the conditional primary interpretation**, with 250, 256 and 512 Hz sensitivities. A near-500 sample count/logged-duration ratio and plausible HR are supporting clues, not a hardware setting: the recording has no timestamp, physical-unit header or verified Chords configuration. The modulo-256 counter checks continuity only. Export each file's sample count, logged seconds, implied ratio, conditional durations, discontinuities and >10% duration-mismatch flag. A mismatch is an audit flag; it is not an automatic exclusion until raw trace and event provenance are reviewed. No samples are synthesized or counter gaps interpolated. Keep the first 5 seconds in the primary run because the investigator described recording onset at eye opening but no event marker exists. Drop 5 seconds only in a labeled sensitivity.

Before QC disposition, generate a queue of raw traces and raw/filtered spectra with channel amplitudes, conditional time axes and the rate assumption printed on the image. Review at least one nominally accepted and one rejected or flagged record per experiment, every rare severe flag, the maximum duration mismatch, and all channel-imbalance clusters. CP-B records owner accept/reject/uncertain decisions and reasons per flagged trial or channel. Automated screening alone cannot certify cortical origin or cardiac validity.

## EEG processing and QC (proposed PD-003)

Retain v1's nonoverlapping 4-second raw epochs, counter-gap exclusion, exact-extrema clipping rules, relative spike/step checks, at least three accepted epochs and accepted fraction ≥0.5. Apply the 1–45 Hz fourth-order Butterworth SOS forward/backward separately to accepted epochs; Welch uses 2-second Hann windows with 50% overlap. Use positive finite integrated theta 4–8 Hz, alpha 8–13 Hz, beta 13–30 Hz and high-frequency 30–45 Hz powers, with no epsilon substitute for a failed band. The existing active specification's detailed thresholds remain in force unless explicitly changed below.

Additional *review flags*, not automatic whole-person exclusions:

| Flag | Proposed reproducible rule | Review consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel imbalance | Ratio of robust raw per-epoch Ch1/Ch2 standard deviations outside [0.5, 2.0], evaluated over paired nonflat, nongap 4-second epochs; use median ratio. | Inspect both channels, contacts and spectra. Do not automatically discard Subj_F or Subj_H. |
| Absolute amplitude anomaly | For each channel, trial median raw-epoch standard deviation <0.1× or >10× the median of the same channel over all nonflat recordings in its experiment. | Possible gain/contact issue; raw unit is unknown, so flag only. Record pooled reference and threshold in export. |
| Residual line noise | On raw, demeaned, gap-free accepted epochs, Welch 48–52 Hz integrated power divided by 1–45 Hz integrated power >0.20. Compute before the 1–45 Hz filter; the filter alone cannot establish line-noise absence. | Inspect raw and cleaned spectrum; mark unresolved contamination separately. |
| High-frequency muscle candidate | On filtered accepted epochs, 30–45 Hz power divided by 1–45 Hz power >0.25. | Inspect for EMG/movement; label as muscle candidate, not cortical beta. |

Ratios use matched PSD units and finite positive denominator; otherwise flag `qc_ratio_unavailable`. Thresholds are operational screeners selected for inspectability, not validated physiological cutoffs. Report threshold sensitivities at half/double the line-noise and muscle ratios and at [0.4, 2.5] for channel imbalance. Do not tune cutoffs to achieve expected participant exclusions or rating correlations. Preserve per-channel flags so a valid single channel can contribute to channel-specific descriptive features, while bilateral FAA requires both accepted channels. For aggregate EEG composite, eligible channels are averaged only after the same rules are applied; a single-channel result is explicitly labeled.

## Candidate features and affect mapping (proposed PD-004)

Compute each candidate on accepted epochs and aggregate by trial median before normalization. Report raw feature and transform provenance:

| Feature | Exact proposed expression | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FAA | `log(alpha_right) - log(alpha_left)` | Approximate forehead log-alpha asymmetry, with existing sign for continuity; valence mapping is a hypothesis. No F3/F4 localization claim. |
| Alpha suppression | `-log(mean(alpha_eligible_channels))` | Cortical arousal candidate. |
| Engagement | `log(mean(beta_eligible_channels) / (mean(alpha_eligible_channels) + mean(theta_eligible_channels)))` | Cortical arousal candidate; this fixes the parentheses missing from the guide's `log β / (α + θ)` shorthand. |
| Ocular activity | `unavailable_detector_not_validated` for this v1.2 proposal | Separate ocular candidate. A later detector needs a separately approved exact event/refractory/motion rule and synthetic plus raw review; do not tune one on these results. |
| Muscle activity | `log(mean(30–45 Hz power over eligible channels))` | Separate forehead EMG candidate, not a cortical arousal component. |
| Cardiac | HR in BPM; log RMSSD in ms only on valid contiguous NN-candidate runs | HR is main cardiac comparison. RMSSD is descriptive and never filled from HR. The active ECG detector's morphology, gap and 10/20/30-second sensitivity rules continue pending CP-B. |

For descriptive coordinates, within each participant **and experiment**, subtract median and divide by `1.4826 × MAD`, requiring ≥3 distinct eligible trials and a positive scale. Apply `tanh` to obtain a bounded signed candidate component. When calibration is undefined, output unavailable with reason. This within-person descriptive mapping uses that person's entire eligible observed distribution and **must not** be used at unseen-person inference. Show alpha suppression and engagement separately. The exploratory EEG arousal composite is their equal mean only when both exist; when one exists, label the single component rather than quietly calling it the composite. Report all four candidate relationships regardless of sign; no candidate is selected by self-report correlation. Blink and EMG never enter the cortical composite.

The proposed **complete physiology point** requires bilateral FAA, both cortical arousal candidates and valid HR. Physiological valence is `tanh(z_FAA)`. Physiological arousal is the equal mean of EEG-composite and transformed HR (`0.5 × EEG + 0.5 × HR`). Negative log RMSSD remains a separate descriptive sensitivity, not a required primary component. A point with EEG but no HR is labeled `partial_EEG`; an HR-only arousal component is `partial_HR`, and an unavailable axis stays unavailable. The complete *fused* point additionally requires both E2/E3 reported axes. Primary fusion is `0.5 × physiology + 0.5 × self_report` **axis by axis**, retaining the v1 equal-weight convention despite the physiology-led presentation. Prespecified α sensitivities are 0.25 and 0.75, plus 0 and 1 endpoints shown explicitly as component endpoints. A partial fusion may be shown descriptively if labeled with its contributors and eligible denominator; it is not silently added to the primary complete target. Owner review may choose a different pre-result primary α, but it must be fixed before dependent analysis and not chosen for model performance.

## Agreement and uncertainty (proposed PD-005)

Export the following E2 and E3 comparisons separately: EEG composite ↔ reported arousal; alpha suppression ↔ reported arousal; engagement ↔ reported arousal; HR ↔ reported arousal; EEG composite ↔ HR; FAA ↔ reported valence; ocular and muscle candidates ↔ reported arousal and HR; and full/partial physiology, reports and fusion differences where both coordinates exist. Report participant-level Spearman ρ and an equal-participant descriptive summary, together with available trials, people and rooms. The exploratory divergence table records opposite-sign within-person standardized arousal components when both magnitudes exceed one robust standard unit. It describes disagreement; it does not establish physiological masking, privacy effects or stress.

Bootstrap uncertainty resamples **participants only**, retaining each selected participant's complete observed room vector and its within-person dependence; use 2,000 seeded draws (seed 2718) only when at least four distinct people contribute. Room identities are fixed as the observed pilot set, so intervals are conditional on those rooms and do not assert coverage for unobserved rooms or a wider participant population. With fewer than four people, print `interval_unavailable_insufficient_people` and individual values. Do not bootstrap 160 trial rows as independent people. Every proposed comparison is exported even when unavailable or null.

## Model questions and fold rules (proposed PD-006)

Evaluate four questions without guaranteed success: (1) room-rating consistency within E2/E3, with E1 comfort separate; (2) transfer of a **known** room's relative self-report profile to a held-out participant; (3) spatial-attribute prediction for a held-out room; (4) physiology/reported-experience agreement. The fourth is the prespecified relationship table, not an opportunistic extra fitted predictor. Historical F1–F10 numbers in the revision guide are exploratory observations to reproduce or correct, never acceptance thresholds.

For (1), fit a crossed additive person+room descriptive decomposition separately by experiment and axis; show residual share and a room-label-within-person permutation negative control (1,000 draws, seed 2718). Enumerate the ten unordered rater partitions for E2 (2/3) and E3 (3/3) for room-rank reliability rather than repeat the same splits as if independent, and state that ten rooms and five/six raters limit precision. For (2), each held-out person's observed mean is used *only to center that person's own ratings for a relative-response evaluation*. Predict the centered room profile using other people, compare to zero baseline, report per-person MAE/Spearman and 1,000 training-room-label negative-control draws. Freeze the held-out person folds and MAE statistic; each draw permutes whole room labels within training people and refits the room profile without touching held-out outcomes. It is not absolute response prediction for an unseen, unobserved person or a calibrated population p-value.

For (3), retain a **fused-target spatial model for the Studio** alongside a self-report-only comparator. The deployable estimand is E3 room response in the source's ten Function rooms, because E3 is the only experiment with the complete independent room inputs used by the current deployed feature schema. E2 lacks walkable floor area and space type; optional `RoomInput` fields are accepted for browsing/API validation but are **not** license to structurally impute a deployable model target. Primary fused training uses E3 rows with raw-QC-eligible bilateral FAA, alpha suppression, engagement, valid HR, both E3 reports and the complete E3 independent spatial attributes. Descriptive within-person calibration availability is **not** a training-cohort gate. A labeled partial-target sensitivity may be evaluated separately, never mixed into the primary training labels. E2 has its own common-feature self-report research analysis; a pooled E2+E3 model with an experiment feature is exploratory **research-only**, because the Studio API has no experiment input and cannot define its deployment estimand. All E1/E2 records remain in eligible nonstudio research analyses. Report the E3-only scope and limitation prominently; do not imply the model generalizes across all thirty rooms. If a cohort cannot support at least four outer-training **room groups** for the room question or four outer-training **person groups** for the person question, use the prespecified median-baseline fallback or export unavailable with exact memberships. The self-report comparator uses all eligible E2/E3 ratings in separate-first research analyses under the same group-isolation rules. Report the target types and cohort sizes side by side; do not replace fused prediction with the comparator.

Use v1's prespecified dummy median/mean, Ridge α={0.1,1,10}, and RandomForest 100 trees with depth {2,4}, leaf {3,5}, seed 2718. **Population target calibration is separate from descriptive mapping:** on each raw training partition, for each of FAA, alpha suppression, engagement and HR, require ≥3 distinct finite eligible values; fit center=median and robust scale=`1.4826 × MAD`; declare scale <`1e-9` uncalibratable rather than flooring it. Transform each raw component by `tanh((x-center)/scale)`, form EEG arousal as the equal mean of transformed alpha suppression and engagement, physiology arousal as `0.5 × EEG + 0.5 × transformed_HR`, physiology valence as transformed FAA, then fused target as `0.5 × physiology + 0.5 × observed_E3_report` per axis. For inner selection, fit these four calibrations **only on inner-training raw rows**; for outer scoring refit on outer-training raw rows. The serialized final artifact includes the full-data E3 calibrator and exact mapping. No held-out person's distribution or held-out room outcome calibrates a fold. Fit feature encoders/scalers and estimator in the same training partition. Outer leave-one-room-out evaluates new E3 rooms; leave-one-person-out is a separate question. Inner `GroupKFold(n_splits=3)` follows the outer question: group by **room** for held-out-room evaluation and by **person** for held-out-person evaluation, never room by default for both. Choose candidates by MAE averaged equally over valence and arousal and equally over the corresponding held-out **room or person groups**, with v1's simplicity tie rule and median-baseline fallback when inner-group/calibration requirements fail; no random-row CV. For the deployed fused artifact, fit its own full-data room-group inner selection independently of outer test scores. Export per-fold memberships, predictions, baselines, eligibility and exact package/spec hashes. Do not report a confidence percentage or error radius without separate calibration evidence.

For a room learning curve, use k=4 through `n_rooms-1` only where at least one test room and four independent training rooms remain. For each k, enumerate or sample without replacement `min(100, C(n_rooms,k))` unique training-room subsets with seed 2718, use the other rooms as test, and perform inner grouped selection within training rooms when feasible; otherwise show baseline-only. Overlapping room subsets create descriptive variability, not independent replications. The spatial-model negative control freezes outer group splits and MAE aggregation equally over valence/arousal and held-out room groups, shuffles **whole room-attribute rows among training-room IDs within each experiment** (not trial rows or held-out attributes), then reruns inner target calibration, feature preprocessing, candidate selection and outer-training refit for each of 1,000 seeded draws where feasible. Report its observed-versus-shuffled score distribution as a descriptive negative control, **not a calibrated population p-value**. If sparse complete fusion makes these designs infeasible, publish the missingness and `unavailable` reason rather than fabricate a trend. Feature importance appears adjacent to model-versus-baseline error and is exploratory.

## Studio model and score contract (proposed PD-007)

One physical `RoomInput` contract and derived feature builder serves fit, CLI, API and bounded generation. The Studio exposes only the **fused** model prediction. Research pages retain physiology, self-report and fusion comparisons. Preserve raw fitted coordinate output; project each axis into [-1,1] only for visual/score use and flag projection. Given projected point `p` and visitor emotional target `t` in [-1,1]², API `neuro_score` stays 0–1 and UI displays `100 × clip(1 - ||p - t||/sqrt(8), 0, 1)`. The owner's requested numerical score `s` is a separate 0–100 input. Generation minimizes `abs(100 × neuro_score(p,t) - s)` over physically valid, empirically supported candidates within user locks and ranges. With s=65, a 64.5 candidate ranks ahead of 95. Use bounded seeded search (seed 2718 by default), deterministic tie break by score difference, then support distance, then stable room-field ordering; never relax locks silently. Expose requested, achieved and absolute difference, feasibility/support, nearest studied room, and explicit empty/unavailable status. A search result is the closest *found* candidate, not a global-optimum guarantee or generated photorealistic render.
