## Evidence boundary and estimands

Original trials, metadata and source checksums are in `data/MANIFEST.sha256`; file-level integrity and rate scenarios are in `artifacts/results/audit.json`. The primary descriptive estimand is the distribution and disagreement of original self-reports and conditionally interpreted physiology during room viewing among these recorded participants. A secondary estimand is predictive error for held-out rooms and held-out participants under specified target-construction assumptions. Neither estimates a causal room effect or validates a clinical/emotion detector. Illumination/CCT are supplied simulation attributes, not measured headset exposure. Experiment 1 comfort remains a distinct descriptive variable; Experiments 2 and 3 differ in rating elicitation and are analyzed separately first. No questionnaire responses beyond supplied ratings are inferred.

## Acquisition time base and cohort

Use 500 Hz as the **conditional primary analysis scenario**, supported by the near-500 median sample/logged-duration ratios but not confirmed hardware settings. Analyze 250, 256 and 512 Hz scenarios for timing- and frequency-dependent sensitivity; report how many trials and features change eligibility. Do not select a rate for favorable affect/model results. Every trial keeps its source filename, actual samples, logged duration, conditional sample duration, modulo-counter discontinuity count and rate status. A >10% sample/logged-duration mismatch is a caveat flag; actual available samples determine processing extent. Counter jumps are flagged and affected windows may be excluded, never repaired by inserted samples. No padding, synthesized baseline, or inferred event timing. The first 5 seconds are retained in primary analysis because there is no event marker justifying onset removal; a sensitivity run drops the first 5 seconds. The shortest file may be ineligible for some derived features.

[Manufacturer Chords-Web documentation](https://docs.upsidedownlabs.tech/software/chords/chords-web/index.html) supports filtering/export possibilities, not this dataset's settings. The [250 Hz firmware variant](https://github.com/upsidedownlabs/NPG-Lite-IDF-Firmware) and [500 Hz Cardio application](https://docs.upsidedownlabs.tech/software/applications/npg-lite-cardio/index.html) show why device-family documentation alone cannot close the rate question. Inspect device settings if later supplied and amend this decision.

## EEG processing and interpretation

Channel1 is owner-reported approximate right forehead; Channel2 is approximate left forehead. Reference/ground wiring and raw amplitude units are unknown. Label alpha output **forehead log-power asymmetry**, `log(alpha_right) - log(alpha_left)`, in log squared-raw-unit ratio. It is not a direct valence measurement or exact Fp2/Fp1 cortical source. Avoid multichannel ICA/source localization with two approximate channels and unknown reference. A [frontal alpha asymmetry methods primer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27865882/) emphasizes reference/artifact/interpretation choices; this project therefore reports the raw component and treats an affect-coordinate mapping as a hypothesis.

At each candidate rate with Nyquist >45 Hz, use SciPy's fourth-order Butterworth 1–45 Hz band-pass in second-order sections, applied forward/backward **separately within each accepted epoch**. The SciPy order-four prototype produces an eighth-order band-pass before the forward/backward pass. Its roll-off attenuates but does not eliminate 50 Hz (about 11 dB forward/backward at 50 Hz for the 500 Hz scenario). Because Chords export filter settings are unknown, do not assume an additional notch or complete mains removal; inspect raw and cleaned spectra for residual 50 Hz contamination. [SciPy Butterworth documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.butter.html) defines order and response.

Raw-sample QC runs **before filtering** on non-overlapping 4-second epochs. A nonfinite sample, flat channel (standard deviation below the configured raw-unit numerical floor), or counter discontinuity entering or inside an epoch rejects it; no filter spans a gap. Repeated exact extrema reveal possible clipping without assuming physical voltage units: reject when either raw channel has at least 10% of epoch samples at one exact extremum with magnitude at least half the channel’s maximum absolute value, or at least 5% at each of its two exact extrema. The magnitude condition avoids mistaking an exact zero baseline in an idealized pulse trace for a clipped rail. Flag `repeated_extrema_possible_clipping`, not proven ADC saturation. Reject extreme raw peak-to-peak or maximum sample-step values over the recording's robust epoch median at configured multiples. This relative rule cannot establish freedom from persistent ocular, muscle or motion contamination across all epochs. Preserve per-epoch reason and coverage. Require at least three accepted epochs (12 seconds) and accepted fraction >=0.5 for primary spectral features. Filter each accepted epoch independently, then use Welch PSD with 2-second Hann segments and 50% overlap, integrate alpha 8–13 Hz and beta 13–30 Hz powers, and aggregate epoch powers by median. Log only finite strictly positive powers; never add an epsilon to make a failed band plausible. Also report log beta/alpha power ratio as a candidate arousal component, with channel order, rate, unresolved amplitude unit and accepted/rejected epoch counts.

The clipping and relative-outlier thresholds are operational screening choices rather than validated artifact removal. Sensitivity varies peak-to-peak/step and repeated-extrema thresholds by the configured factors, as well as rate and first-5-second exclusion. Synthetic tests must recover known bands and reject sustained clipping, isolated spikes, flats and gaps. Before interpreting results, inspect raw/cleaned traces and spectra for one accepted and one rejected/flagged trial per experiment when available, every rare clipping flag and the worst duration mismatch. Record those review choices and unresolved contamination. [The MNE epoch-quality example](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_examples/preprocessing/plot_epoch_quality.html) motivates inspectable quality features but does not validate this dataset's thresholds.

## ECG processing and interpretation

Channel3 is owner-reported wrist ECG; lead configuration, voltage units and acquisition filtering are unverified. Apply the same repeated-extrema rule as EEG to consecutive one-second raw blocks; exclude and split at any flagged possible-clipping block. A one-second raw block with standard deviation below the configured floor is a dropout and is likewise excluded. Reject nonfinite recordings. Split at each modulo-counter discontinuity and exclude flat dropout blocks. No filtering, RR interval or RMSSD difference crosses a split; no synthetic samples or interpolation repair the gap. Filter each remaining segment of at least two seconds separately using a fourth-order 5–25 Hz SOS band-pass with forward/backward application when Nyquist permits.

For each segment and each polarity, SciPy `find_peaks` uses minimum separation 0.30 s and prominence at least half the 95th percentile of absolute filtered amplitude. A candidate polarity is admitted only when median absolute peak amplitude divided by median absolute filtered amplitude is >=5, median correlation of ±0.08 s peak-centered normalized morphologies with their median template is >=0.8, and at least 80% of those peaks have template correlation >=0.7. All thresholds are operational hypotheses fixed before real analysis. Rank admitted polarities by peak/background ratio × median template correlation × fraction of RR intervals in 0.30–2.0 s; reject `ambiguous_polarity` when the best score exceeds the other by less than 5%. If no polarity passes, reject `no_qrs_quality`. Noise-only, clipped, dropout and inverted/fragmented synthetic signals are required tests. [SciPy peak finding](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.find_peaks.html) detects numerical extrema, not established R waves.

Within each continuous segment, candidate RR intervals must be 0.30–2.0 s and within 25% of the median of the neighboring two intervals on each side (excluding the interval itself; use available neighbors at edges). Peaks failing morphology break an interval. Keep raw peak indices, RR values, morphology/quality metrics, validity masks and gap reasons. Never concatenate disjoint intervals. Select the **longest single contiguous valid interval run** by covered seconds, with earlier start breaking ties. Heart rate requires at least six beats (five intervals) and at least five seconds of usable coverage; compute `60 / median(accepted RR seconds)` for that run. Primary RMSSD requires a qualifying single run with >=20 consecutive intervals **and >=30 seconds covered by those intervals**, then uses the successive differences within that run only, in milliseconds. If no run qualifies, RMSSD is unavailable even when the source file is longer than 30 seconds. The 20-second sensitivity requires >=12 intervals and >=20 seconds of one run; the 10-second sensitivity requires >=6 intervals and >=10 seconds. These complete duration/count rules are evaluated on the same QC output, never by pooling across gaps. Short, invalid or ambiguous channels retain explicit reason codes; no nominal HR, 50 ms RMSSD or neutral target is emitted. Accepted RR values are an automated operational approximation to NN intervals, not verified normal beats.

[Ultra-short HRV validation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26414314/) and [a 30-second reliability study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25807067/) found duration-dependent agreement in their own controlled settings; they do not validate motion-prone wrist recordings in standing VR. Per-trial representative raw/filtered peak and gap review precedes interpretation. ECG remains independently valid/invalid from EEG.

## Constructed affect and missingness

Keep original transformed comfort/valence/arousal unchanged. Objective valence candidate uses only the signed forehead log-alpha asymmetry; objective arousal candidate uses EEG log beta/alpha and ECG heart-rate/negative log-RMSSD components when valid. No component is by itself a validated affect axis. For descriptive summaries, standardize available components within participant (minimum three valid trials and nonzero robust scale); this explicitly uses all of a participant's trial distribution and cannot be used as a deployment transform. Map component z scores through `tanh(z)` into [-1,1], with equal arousal component weights among **declared available components**. Full objective requires EEG and both ECG components; partial objective must be labelled with the exact contributing modalities. A missing objective or self-report axis stays missing. Full fusion on an axis exists only when both that axis's objective and self-report are present, `fused = alpha*objective + (1-alpha)*self_report`. Primary alpha=0.5 gives equal descriptive weighting, with 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 sensitivity. Objective-only, subjective-only and partial-modality records are separate categories, never silently pooled into a complete-fusion target. A self-report-free trial has no invented animation starting point. Experiment 1 comfort is never used as valence. Experiment 2/3 comparisons are stratified; pooled results, if shown, are explicitly exploratory and carry an experiment indicator. Compare component/fusion disagreements on the common constructed coordinates with available-pair counts.

For held-out evaluation, use a **population component transform fitted on each training partition only**. For each raw component, the center is the training median and scale is `1.4826 × training median absolute deviation`; require at least three distinct finite training values and scale >=1e-9, otherwise that component is uncalibratable and its dependent target is unavailable in that fold. Apply the frozen center/scale and `tanh` mapping to both training and held-out raw components. The held-out participant's component distribution is never used to fit a transform. When inner model selection partitions outer training data, refit component transforms on every **inner-training** partition before making its inner-validation targets; do not construct targets once on the full outer training data. Descriptive within-person calibration is separate and never a supervised target. A new-person prediction refers to population-constructed coordinates, not a personalized response. Tests mutate held-out components/outcomes and verify training transform and model settings are unchanged.

## Dependence, inference and model evaluation

Report trial, participant, room, experiment and valid-modality counts together. Participant and room are crossed/repeated; the small 10-person/30-room pilot does not support default trial-iid confidence intervals. Primary associations are descriptive room and participant summaries with no p-values or causal language. No inferential interval is emitted for a measure without a justified unit and sufficient independent groups. If participant-cluster bootstrap summaries are added, state they condition on the observed rooms and do not solve crossed room dependence; avoid a falsely universal 95% interval. Singular mixed models and absent interval-calibration data yield `unavailable` with reasons, not adjusted thresholds after looking at results.

Primary full-attribute model eligibility is complete-fusion trials with all independent room attributes, expected mainly in Experiment 3; report actual exclusions and do not treat Experiment 1 comfort as an axis. Any Experiment 2 reduced-feature analysis is a separately labelled sensitivity with its own schema, never silent imputation of structural space type/walkable area. Use held-out-room and held-out-participant `LeaveOneGroupOut` as **separate** generalization questions; repeated subject IDs share one subject group. Each outer training partition performs its own inner `GroupKFold(n_splits=3)` using the **same group type** as the outer question. Inner selection is available only with at least four training groups; otherwise fit a fixed training-median baseline, mark selection unavailable, and still evaluate the held-out fold if targets are constructible. `LeaveOneGroupOut` ensures group separation but not experiment comparability ([scikit-learn documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.LeaveOneGroupOut.html)).

Frozen candidate configurations are training-mean and training-median `DummyRegressor`, Ridge with `alpha ∈ {0.1,1,10}`, and RandomForest with 100 trees, `max_depth ∈ {2,4}`, `min_samples_leaf ∈ {3,5}`, `max_features=1.0`, seed 2718 and one job. Every numeric imputer (training median plus missingness indicators), one-hot categorical encoder (`handle_unknown=ignore`), scaler where used, component target transform and estimator are fitted in the inner-training partition; no outer or inner validation feature/target distribution fits a transformer. For each inner validation group, calculate valence MAE and arousal MAE and average them equally; average group scores equally rather than weighting large groups more. A candidate with invalid target calibration in any inner fold is ineligible and its reason reported. Choose the lowest average; differences <=1e-6 are ties, resolved toward the simpler candidate in this fixed order: median baseline, mean baseline, Ridge alpha 10/1/0.1, then RF by smaller depth and larger leaf. This rule makes baseline preference deterministic, not result-dependent judgement. Refit the selected configuration and target transform on the entire outer-training partition, evaluate its untouched outer group and both baselines, and record memberships/per-axis MAE. R² is reported only where defined. The outer results assess the **selection procedure** and model weakness; no winner is selected by outer test scores.

For the final artifact, repeat the same three-fold room-group inner selection on the **full eligible dataset** (or fixed median baseline if fewer than four room groups), then fit its component transform and selected model on all eligible records. The target transform parameters, feature order, model configuration, data/spec hashes and inner selection evidence travel with the artifact. Use grouped outer error only to describe generalization/weakness, not to change that final configuration. A selected baseline is labelled `baseline_only`; another selected model with weak outer results is labelled `not_better_than_baseline` without withholding the experimental demonstrator. If no fold can construct a complete target or no valid fit exists, record model `unavailable` with reasons. No calibrated prediction interval or heuristic confidence percentage is emitted without separate calibration evidence. [Scikit-learn pipeline guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/compose.html), [common pitfalls](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html) and [nested evaluation example](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/model_selection/plot_nested_cross_validation_iris.html) motivate fold-contained transforms and untouched outer evaluation.

The score is `clip(1 - EuclideanDistance(point, user_target)/sqrt(8), 0, 1)` for finite signed coordinates in [-1,1]. Raw model outputs outside that square are explicitly projected coordinate-wise before scoring and flagged; raw prediction values remain available for auditing. Space type constrains room candidates but does not select the emotional target. Optimizer defaults sample only independent variables within observed feasible support; nearest-observed multivariate distance/support is labelled separately from physical validity. Seeded bounded search can return empty. The saved artifact includes complete fitted preprocessing/model, feature schema, dependency/specification/data hashes, metrics and limitations. Fitting never happens at API startup or request time.
