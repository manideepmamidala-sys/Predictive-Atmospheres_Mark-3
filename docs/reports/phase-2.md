The source inventory remained verified. `artifacts/results/signals_detail.json` contains source-linked primary results, reason codes, original peak and RR evidence, four rate scenarios, first-five-second and EEG threshold sensitivities, and bounded raw/filtered windows for all 160 anchored trials. EEG acceptance is an epoch-count screen, not evidence of artifact-free signals. It rejected 38 counter-discontinuous epochs, six extreme peak-to-peak epochs and five abrupt-step epochs; these reasons can coexist in a trial. Every trial still exceeded the frozen minimum three accepted epochs and 50% accepted fraction. The first-five-second exclusion retained EEG eligibility for 159 trials; either declared relative-threshold or clipping-threshold perturbation retained 160. No primary EEG epoch triggered repeated-extrema possible-clipping. These screens cannot remove persistent ocular, muscle or motion contamination. Per-record ECG reason occurrences include 78 `no_qrs_quality`, 64 insufficient contiguous primary RMSSD coverage, 18 counter-discontinuity flags, 18 short-continuous-segment flags, 11 insufficient contiguous HR beat runs, and one flat-dropout flag; reasons can coexist and some indicate partial segments rather than total invalidity. HR remains separately available from RMSSD.

At the same samples, the 250/256/500/512 Hz scenarios yielded respectively 31/31/18/19 primary RMSSD-eligible trials, while EEG eligibility stayed 160 at every rate. At the conditional 500 Hz rate, the complete 10/20/30-second single-run ECG scenarios yielded 52/25/18 eligible trials. These differences make the unverified acquisition rate and usable duration consequential; the 500 Hz primary choice was fixed before signal processing and was not changed to improve eligibility. The shortest recorded file has 7,961 samples, or 15.922 seconds conditional on 500 Hz, against 60 logged seconds. Its 30-second RMSSD is correctly unavailable.

The reproducible `pa signal-review` command rendered ten [representative inspection panels](signal-review/index.json): one accepted and one no-QRS-quality ECG example per experiment where available, one EEG flagged example per experiment, and the largest duration mismatch. The panels show original Ch1/Ch2/Ch3 windows, accepted-epoch filtered EEG spectra, segment-filtered ECG and admitted candidate peaks. The ECG plot title distinguishes **the displayed segment** from whole-record QC flags; an admitted segment can coexist with a dropout or counter flag elsewhere. Source-amplitude units and seconds remain explicitly conditional.

Visual inspection found clear sharp, recurring ECG complexes in accepted E2 `Subj_M/Rm_013` and E3 `Subj_B/Rm_029` windows, but this short review does not verify all automated beat labels as normal-to-normal intervals. Rejected E2 `Subj_E/Rm_013` also has visible sharp deflections, illustrating that the conservative morphology rule can exclude plausible beats. E3 `Subj_B/Rm_029` has a large bilateral low-frequency EEG transient in an automatically accepted epoch, consistent with an unresolved shared ocular/motion possibility; other accepted examples also show slow deflections. The flagged E2 `Subj_M/Rm_014` and E3 `Subj_H/Rm_024` EEG windows contain large transients and were excluded as specified. Several raw spectra show a narrow dip near 50 Hz, but export/acquisition filtering is unverified, so its cause cannot be identified and residual contamination remains possible. No rejection or mapping threshold was changed after seeing these outcomes.

These panels are selected examples, not exhaustive manual annotation. The 18 primary RMSSD records and 160 EEG feature records remain **operational candidates** with visual-review and acquisition limitations. Any later affect coordinate using them is constructed and exploratory. The software retains rejected/missing values as null with reasons rather than nominal physiology.
