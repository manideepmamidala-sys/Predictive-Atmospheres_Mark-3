## Why an amendment is needed

The thesis booklet's objective/subjective/fused density fields and Design Studio are central to the owner's research design, but its reported baseline, acquisition rate, broad questionnaires, validated physiological valence and model confidence are not verified by the supplied records. The corrected protocol and current specification distinguish observed data from historical claims. Current code's descriptive `complete_fusion` requires FAA, beta/alpha, HR and RMSSD; the population model calibrator likewise requires all four. This produced a narrow historical model cohort, and any change to the primary cardiac component or fusion point is a real target/cohort change, not a visual relabel. The owner wants EEG/ECG-led comparative analysis and a fused-only Studio, with numerical weights and missingness settled before dependent analysis.

The draft retains conditional 500 Hz and a first-five-seconds-included primary timeline. Counter continuity and rate plausibility are supporting evidence, not sample-clock confirmation. It adds review-only channel ratio [0.5,2], within-experiment raw amplitude 0.1×/10× median, raw 48–52 Hz line-noise ratio >0.20 and cleaned 30–45 Hz muscle-power ratio >0.25, all with explicit calculation stages. It proposes alpha suppression `-log(alpha)`, engagement `log(beta/(alpha+theta))`, FAA with unchanged sign, and a within-person/experiment exploratory cortical composite. Blink-like events and muscle power stay distinct from cortical activity. The owner-signed CP-B queue, not a favorable expected result, governs exclusions.

For a complete physiology point, the proposal requires bilateral FAA, both EEG arousal candidates and valid HR; RMSSD stays descriptive. Complete fused rows also require both E2/E3 report axes. EEG-only and HR-only points remain labeled partial. Physiology arousal averages transformed EEG composite and HR equally; fused axes use α=0.5 physiology and 0.5 report, with fixed α sensitivities. Descriptive points use within-person/experiment scaling; model targets use a separate raw-component population calibrator fitted inside each training partition. This preserves separate physiology, report and fusion findings while retaining the active equal-fusion convention for a proposed primary target. Owner emphasis on physiology does not, by itself, determine α.

Models would report room-rating consistency, known-room relative profile transfer, held-out-room spatial prediction for fused and self-report targets, and physiology/report agreement, with independent group counts, fold-fitted transforms, baselines, declared negative controls and unavailable states. The guide's prior percentages and signs are exploratory observations to reproduce, not goals. The proposed deployed fused model is **E3 only**, because E2 has no walkable floor area/space type and the API has no experiment input. E2 remains in its eligible research analyses; exploratory pooled models are not Studio artifacts. The Studio prediction therefore cannot claim generalization across all thirty rooms. The score is 0–100 target proximity, and generation minimizes distance from the *requested numerical score* subject to room locks and physical/studied support. This is the owner's accepted product behavior, not a new question.
