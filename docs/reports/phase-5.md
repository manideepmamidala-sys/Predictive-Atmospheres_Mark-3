# Phase 5 — grouped experimental model evaluation

Date: 2026-10-08. `pa train` used the frozen analysis v1.1.0 method after the recorded independent checkpoint PASS. `artifacts/results/model_evaluation.json` retains eligible identities, exclusion reasons, every outer membership, every inner calibration/score where available, candidate choices, baseline comparisons and the final full-data selection. The fitted `artifacts/model/model.joblib` is a **weak experimental demonstrator**, not an emotion predictor established for deployment decisions. No descriptive within-person target normalization entered this evaluation.

The primary full-attribute cohort contains **14 Experiment 3 trials from three participants and ten rooms** with original valence/arousal, operational EEG/ECG components and all room attributes. Of 60 Experiment 3 trials, 24 lack both heart rate and primary RMSSD, and 22 lack RMSSD while retaining heart rate. The other 100 trials are from Experiments 1/2 and were not silently pooled into this full-attribute cohort. These 14 raw-component records differ from the ten Experiment 3 **descriptive complete-fusion** records: model targets use population medians/MADs fitted only within training partitions and can include a participant with fewer than three within-person RMSSD observations.

MAE uses equal weight for the two constructed axes and equal weight per held-out group, not trial-iid weighting. Room-holdout procedure MAE is **worse than the median baseline** by 0.0204 coordinate units. Only four of ten room folds have at least two variable held-out targets for per-axis R²; undefined values remain null. The three participant-holdout folds have only two training participant groups each, below the frozen minimum four for three-fold inner selection, so they correctly fit the fixed median baseline. They do not test a tuned model on unseen people. Per-fold results and both baseline axis errors are retained in the JSON; there are no confidence intervals or significance tests.

Independent full-data **room-group** inner selection on the 14 eligible records chose RandomForestRegressor with 100 trees, depth 2, minimum leaf 3 and seed 2718. Its inner group-weighted MAE was 0.5208; the training-mean baseline's was 0.5223 and median baseline's 0.5682. This final selection was not chosen by outer scores. The pipeline then fitted preprocessing, the selected estimator and population target calibration on all 14 records, preserving centers/scales and source/specification hashes in artifact metadata. Despite that inner choice, the actual untouched room-holdout procedure did not beat the median baseline, so artifact/model status is **`not_better_than_baseline`**. The simulator can illustrate a bounded prediction and support warning, but its score is a mathematical coordinate distance, not validated architectural quality or user well-being.

The model's effective participant diversity is especially thin: ten records are from one participant, and two each from the other two. Acquisition time base, ECG beat labels, EEG artifact rejection, emotional mapping and simulation-to-headset exposure remain unresolved. Room and participant holdouts address different questions and neither repairs those measurement limitations. No post-result candidate, threshold, target weight or rate adjustment was made.
