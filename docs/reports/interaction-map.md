# Legacy interaction map

This map compares the former Streamlit tabs in `frontend/app.py` and controls in `frontend/ui/` with the seven-route React site. Historical figures are retained under `docs/history/legacy-site-assets/` as evidence of the former presentation, not reused as validated results. Research pages now read generated, schema-validated exports; prediction and search alone call the live API.

| Former view or control | New route and behavior | Decision / evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Landing Page | **The study** explains the question, protocol, source provenance, effective counts and limits; its circumplex shows exported eligible positions with an accessible table. | Trial and contributor counts come from the research bundle. No fixed headline result or DOI is invented. |
| Design Studio geometry, openings, light and space controls | **Room simulator** has typed physical inputs and explicit user-selected target valence/arousal. It sends independent variables to `/v1/predict`; service validation owns derived features and support status. | Total door/window areas retain total-area semantics. Blank optional observations remain missing; invalid geometry is rejected by the API. |
| Design Studio “Auto-Design Room” and fixed room-type alignment | **Room simulator** “Suggest rooms” sends the chosen target and optional categorical restrictions to `/v1/optimize`, showing seeded bounded candidates with dimensions, use and lighting or an empty/unavailable reason. | Space type no longer sets the emotional target; a user-selected target replaces the legacy fixed centroid/heuristic confidence. Nearest studied room imagery is labelled as a neighbor, not a visualization of the proposed candidate. |
| Design Studio emotion profile and visual plots | **Room simulator** reports prediction, target-proximity score, support and model limitations beside a rotatable geometric massing schematic; **Affect** provides the shared valence–arousal plane. | Historical profile bars and pseudo-confidence are retired pending validated comparable evidence. The schematic represents entered dimensions, not a simulation of light or measured emotion. |
| Human Metrics raw aggregate charts and dataframe | **Signals** selects an experiment and trial, toggles bounded raw/cleaned EEG and ECG traces, shades rejected segments, and lists EEG feature, heart-rate and primary RMSSD availability separately with reasons, band power and FAA. | The trace product loads only on Signals. Anti-aliased 100 Hz browser traces are labelled separately from the conditional 500 Hz analysis scenario; full-rate source evidence remains in the research pipeline. The browser does not calculate physiological features. |
| Spatial Insights and Environmental Impacts filters, profiles, day/night charts | **Rooms** filters source-matched renders by experiment, recorded lighting and documented space type; a keyboard operable panorama, per-room metadata, up to three-room comparison and eligible room positions support inspection. | Missing CCT/day/night and early-experiment fields remain unavailable. Legacy correlations, feature effects and environmental plots require validated backend products; historical static images are not published as new findings. |
| Emotion Landscape free 3D feature axes and valence/arousal maps | **Affect** switches self-report, mapped physiology and fused coordinates on one accessible plane and table, with experiment/participant filters. | Unvalidated 3D projections are retired. No plotted origin is assigned where self-report or a complete coordinate is absent. |
| Affective Fusion data-layer radio and density | **Affect** retains the three component switch, separates complete from partial-modality fusion, displays contributing participant counts and filters exported α-sensitivity summaries by experiment and weight. | α selection inspects complete-fusion precomputed summaries; it does not falsely recompute per-trial positions in the browser. Open markers identify partial-modality points. |
| Demographic Insights gender, age and sleep radio | **People** lists participant IDs, eligible counts and available demographic/sleep metadata with experiment/ID filters. | The export does not support a defensible demographic effect chart for the sparse independent groups. Structural sleep absence outside Experiment 3 is shown as unavailable. |
| Model Training architecture choice, legacy PyTorch toggle and retrain button | **Model report** is read-only: exported held-out/baseline metrics, eligibility, intervals when defensible, artifact status and limitations. | Training is a reproducible backend command, never a public route or page control. The old retrain button was disabled, and legacy torch models are removed from the active runtime. |
| System Architecture tab | Replaced by public methods, cards and repository documentation linked from the study/report context. | Intake explicitly removes this tab. |

The atlas exposes missing and weak-model states. The final browser pass uses the generated 160-trial bundle: only 14 positions across two people have complete descriptive fusion, while 96 positions use partial modalities. The model report shows `not_better_than_baseline` and the simulator keeps that status visible even when the fitted service responds.
